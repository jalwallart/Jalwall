Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions homeowners ask before installing a bubble wall in their living space.

Will a bubble wall fit in a normal living room?

Yes. Bubble walls can be designed for different wall sizes, making them suitable for both compact apartments and larger homes.

Most residential installations are customised to fit a feature wall without overwhelming the space.

Does a bubble wall make noise?

Bubble walls are designed to operate quietly so they do not disturb the comfort of your home.

Instead of noise, they create a gentle visual movement that adds a relaxing atmosphere to the room.

Can the lighting colours be changed?

Yes. Modern bubble walls use LED lighting that allows different colour effects.

You can choose colours that match your interior design or create different moods for your space.

Is maintenance complicated for homeowners?

Bubble walls are built for long-term use with minimal maintenance.

Once installed properly, they require only basic care to keep the water clear and the system running smoothly.
